Core Architecture

Designed for zero digital distraction

Traditional browsers waste vertical screen space with tabs and buttons. Prism reimagines the layout for widescreen focus.

Vertical Tabs

Read page titles completely without squeezing tabs into unreadable icons. Collapse to an ultra-thin rail with Alt+B.

Native Tracker Blocking

Integrated EasyList and EasyPrivacy engines eliminate ad-trackers, telemetry scripts, and malware vectors before they resolve.

Zero Local Telemetry

Your history, bookmarks, passwords, and private AI keys never leave your machine. No mandatory accounts, no cloud sync traps.

AI Copilot

Your multi-model companion in the browser

Prism connects directly to your preferred AI provider. Extract insights from research papers, translate languages, or draft responses with live webpage context.

Speed & Control

Keyboard-driven efficiency

Keep your hands on the keys. Every core navigation workflow has an ergonomic shortcut.

Shortcut Action Description
Alt + B Toggle Sidebar Instantly collapse or expand the vertical tab bar into a minimalist rail.
Ctrl + T / Alt + T New Tab Launch the ambient greeting home view with instant search.
Ctrl + W Close Tab Close the current active view.
Ctrl + Tab Cycle Tabs Fluidly switch through open workspaces and tabs.
Alt + D Downloads Drawer Slide out the download management drawer.
Alt + , Settings Modal Access theme, AI API key configuration, and search engine settings.
